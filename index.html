This demo needs WebGPU

No WebGPU device is available here, so the depth model has nowhere to run. Everything happens locally on your GPU — there's no server-side fallback to switch to.

Either the browser doesn't support WebGPU, or it does but couldn't get hold of a GPU adapter — which also happens on older or blocklisted graphics drivers.
